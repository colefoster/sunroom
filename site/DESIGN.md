# Sunroom visual world

A straightforward page for a free macOS utility. Keep the dark graphite canvas, off-white type, peach accents and rounded Outfit wordmark/headings from Sunroom. Use DM Sans for readable explanations and controls. Accent marks emphasis and selection; it is not a repeated sales button.

The page explains what the utility does, lets visitors try one generous interactive example, and gives inline source-build instructions. No repeated product pitches, conversion banners, install modal or duplicate mini-demo. Free/current source-build availability is stated up front. Setup remains on the dark background.

Adopt Safelight’s explicit lock-state explanations: Unlocked / Move & resize and Locked / Click through. Show is independent. Provide real click-through feedback and larger, readable control labels. Always-on-top and Spaces are native-app features, not simulated website controls.

The demo’s bright shared content occupies roughly one-third of a dark browser/terminal scene. The overlay starts unlocked, visibly offset and differently sized from the shared window, with a draggable title bar and resize corner. It never tracks or snaps to the window. Controls follow the scene immediately on mobile; navigation remains available.

Local fonts, no external runtime dependencies. Native app unchanged. Earlier standalone variants and blind-comparison evidence remain preserved separately.
