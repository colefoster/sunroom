Sunroom 0.1.0 — macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel

Drag Sunroom.app to Applications. Eject this disk image, then open Sunroom
from Applications. Look for the sun icon in your menu bar; there is no Dock icon.

This initial release is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.
macOS may block the first launch because Apple has not verified its developer.
If you trust this download, try opening it once, then open System Settings >
Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Confirm the prompt. Managed Macs
may not permit this. Do not disable Gatekeeper to install Sunroom.

Use Unlock · Move & Resize to place the tint box. Return to the sun icon and
choose Lock · Click Through when done. Show/hide is independent of locking.
Quit from the sun menu to stop the overlay.
