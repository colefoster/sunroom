You’ve made your Mac comfortable. Then someone shares a bright spreadsheet. Sunroom shades the shared window, so you can keep following along without dimming everything else.

01

Place your box.

Unlock the tint box, drag its top bar, and resize it from the corner. Position it yourself over whatever is bright — it doesn’t follow or snap to a window.

02

Set the atmosphere.

Start with Amber, Blue or Gray. Choose any RGB color and dial the opacity from 5% to 80%.

03

Carry on as usual.

Lock the box to click through to the app underneath. Show or hide it separately, without losing the size and position you set.

sunroom.

A fresh face for Sunglasses, a macOS menu-bar utility.

Made for your corner of the screen.

A little setup.
Then a little shade.

Sunroom is the website’s name for Sunglasses. The native app still appears as Sunglasses on your Mac.

There isn’t a packaged download here yet. Build the app from its source with Xcode on macOS 13 or later.

  1. Open the source repository ↗ and download or clone it.
  2. Open Sunglasses.xcodeproj in Xcode.
  3. Select the Sunglasses scheme and My Mac, then choose Product → Run.
  4. Look for the sun icon in your menu bar.

If Xcode requests signing, select your own development team in Signing & Capabilities.
