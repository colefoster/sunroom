## Product truth and requirements
This is a small macOS menu-bar app for a manually positioned, resizable tinted rectangle. The owner runs dark mode everywhere but other people share bright light-mode windows on Zoom/Teams. The box shades a selected area, without changing the rest of the dark workspace. It does not automatically find, follow, snap to, or convert a window to dark mode.

Visibility and lock are separate. Show/hide preserves geometry. Unlocked exposes a draggable title bar and resize handle; locked makes the tint ignore mouse input so the app underneath can be used. Color, 5–80% opacity, Amber/Blue/Gray presets, always-on-top, all Spaces and saved local settings are native features. Default tint RGB .15/.1/0 at 30%; Amber preset .2/.12/0 at 30%, Blue 0/.05/.2 at 25%, Gray .05/.05/.05 at 35%. macOS 13+. No packaged download verified; honest Xcode/source instructions are appropriate.

The page must be dark by default. A bright shared screen occupies roughly a third of each demo while the rest is dark browser/terminal/background. Each tint illustration must actually work and obviously be an independent offset box, sized differently from the window underneath. Dark-mode readability, responsive design, clear controls and truthful copy matter.
